# Study Design

## Participants
- 71 recruited (32 ASD, 39 TD). Final analysed sample: **28 autistic adults** (20–44 y, M = 29.8, SD = 7.1; 16 M / 12 F) and **31 TD adults** (19–41 y, M = 28.4, SD = 5.6; 14 M / 17 F).
- ASD: confirmed ICD-10 diagnosis (F84.5, F84.0, or F84.1), no comorbid neurological conditions; recruited from autism outpatient clinic database and local autism network.
- TD: no neurological/psychiatric conditions, no psychotropic medication.
- Exclusions: 8 TD (elevated RADS-R indicating autism-like traits, or incomplete eye-tracking) and 4 ASD (incomplete/poor-quality eye-tracking).
- Groups matched on age, vision (Landolt), colour vision (Ishihara), IQ (CFT-20-R), d2 attention; differed on BDI-II, RADS-R, IU, STAI (see Table 1).
- Power: PANGEA v0.2, based on d = 0.33 (Allenmark et al., 2021) → 24/group for 90% power, α = .05, to detect the Group × Prevalence interaction.

## Apparatus
- Viewing distance 57 cm with chinrest; Dell UltraSharp U2412M 24" LCD, 1920×1200 px, 60 Hz.
- EyeLink 1000 Desktop-mounted eye tracker (right eye), PsychoPy 2022.2.2.

## Stimuli
- Fixation: grey circle (0.5°), black cross (0.5°×0.5°), central grey dot (0.1°).
- Cue: white line (2°×0.5°) indicating the potential singleton-distractor location with 100% validity.
- Search display: six coloured items at six equidistant locations 6° from centre — one diamond target (3°×3°, contains horizontal/vertical line) and five non-diamond shapes (circle/triangle/pentagon/hexagon, oblique lines). Singleton = single discrepant-colour item; target/singleton colours alternate cyan/orange. Absent trials use uniform colour.

## Design (factors)
- **Group** (between): ASD vs TD.
- **Distractor Prevalence** (within, session-wise): low (50% singleton presence) vs high (90%), two counterbalanced sessions.
- **Singleton presence** (within, trial-wise): absent vs present.
- **Cue validity**: fixed at 100% (cue points to a random location on singleton-absent trials).

## Procedure
- Each session: 10 blocks × 20 trials = 200 trials; two sessions → **400 trials/participant**. Ten practice trials before each session.
- Trial: fixation cross (500 ms) → line cue alongside fixation (500 ms; instructed to ignore) → search display until keyboard response → jittered ITI 1.0–1.5 s.
- Response: identify the diamond's internal line orientation — horizontal = left arrow, vertical = up arrow; speed and accuracy emphasised; block-wise accuracy feedback.
- Eye-tracker: standard 5-point calibration + validation; recalibration before session 2.

## Measures
- **Behavioural**: RT (correct trials), accuracy, derived distractor interference (present − absent).
- **Eye movements** (1.5° ROIs around each location): first-fixation landing (target/distractor/other), number of fixations per trial, target-location and distractor-location refixations.
